Gmail Outcome Handling

A late rejection should update the original tracker row, even when it arrives months after the application.

Subject: Update on your Chief of Staff application
Date: 2026-08-23
From: Priya Shah

Thanks for your interest. We have decided not to move forward.
  • Match the email to the existing ExampleCo row.
  • Set the outcome date and status.
  • Stop follow-ups.
  • Do not create a duplicate lead.

Quality Bar

Bad output says "message leadership" or "contact hiring team." Good output names the person, explains why they are credible, cites the source signal, names the risk, and gives a draft that can be edited and sent.
